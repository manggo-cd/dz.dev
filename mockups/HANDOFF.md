## SOLVED (v10) — the home cyber-girl image
Current candidate is now **`mockups/v10.html`** (built from v9). The recurring "it's stuck in the middle / between the navbar lengths" bug and the new-image integration are fixed.

**Root cause of "stuck in the middle":** the `.hero-girl` was `position:fixed; right:0` but lived INSIDE `.stage`/`.view`. The page-load `animation: enter` on `.view.on` animates `transform`, which makes `.view` a *containing block* — so the fixed girl anchored to the 600px content column instead of the viewport. (v3 worked precisely because v3's `.view.on` has NO animation.) **Fix:** the girl now lives at `<body>` level (sibling of `.bg`), so it pins to the true viewport right edge. It's shown only on home via `body[data-view="home"] .hero-girl{opacity:1}`, set by `go()`.

**Root cause of "pasted light block" / "jagged cut":** `image copy 6.png` is high-key (cyan face on white paper). Knocking the white bg out to transparent left a jagged hair silhouette he disliked. **Final fix (v10 = Option C, duotone melt):** don't cut it — recolour it. Two sources crossfade with the theme (two stacked `<img>`, `.g-dark`/`.g-light`, opacity by `html[data-theme]`):
- DARK: `assets/cyber6-duo.png` — the girl mapped to a dark cyber duotone (highlights crushed so the white glare is gone) so she dissolves into the dark bg like v3's phase.png. No cut, no jagged edge.
- LIGHT: `assets/cyber6-tight.png` — the bright original, which melts into the light bg on its own.
Edges are handled by a soft left+bottom fade mask on `.hero-girl` (uniform gradient, never follows the hair). Assets are regenerated by `mockups/assets/_assets.py` (`python3 _assets.py`).

**v11 = saved variant (Option D, the cyber eye):** `mockups/v11.html` is v10 with the home image swapped to `assets/cyber-eye.png` (`image copy 3`, the dark eye with △○✕□ reflected, PS2 wording cropped). Naturally dark → melts effortlessly. He liked it and wanted it kept for later; do NOT delete.

**Home image CSS (v10):** `position:fixed; right:0; width:min(52vw,600px); height:100vh`; multi-direction fade via `mask-image` + `mask-composite:intersect` (left fade toward text, gentle right/top/bottom fades so it clears the nav and melts into the grid/footer). Nav links have a `text-shadow` bg-colored halo so they stay readable over her at any width. `@media(max-width:900px)` dims her (`opacity:.5`) with a bigger top fade so she sits behind the copy on narrow windows. Verified at 1440 + 620 widths in both themes.
